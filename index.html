1Goal

The goal of this pilot is to prove that an AI agent can assist with the initial estimating workflow by handling routine customer inquiries, gathering missing information, and preparing a draft estimate for human review.

This pilot is intentionally narrow. It is designed to validate the concept before expanding into additional products, inboxes, locations, or workflows.

What we want to prove

An AI agent can take a business card request from the first email to a draft estimate in Corebridge, without asking customers for things they already told us, and without making decisions that belong to AlphaGraphics staff.

2What the pilot covers

For the initial pilot, AltOps will focus on one standardized product category, beginning with business cards. The agent will be designed to do the following.

Read and understand the request

  • Review test customer estimate requests and relevant email thread history.
  • Identify the product being requested and the information already provided.
  • Determine what additional information is required.

Ask for what is missing

  • Ask concise and appropriate follow-up questions.
  • Ask relevant outcome-based questions where useful.
  • Maintain context across multiple customer responses.
  • Avoid asking customers for information they have already provided.
  • Recognize requests that are too complex or uncertain and flag them for human review.

Create the draft estimate

  • Access Corebridge to test the process of creating a draft estimate.
  • Where practical, search for a comparable historical estimate or order and use it as the starting point for the new estimate.
  • Leave pricing decisions, subjective production decisions, discounts, and final approval to an AlphaGraphics employee.

3How we will test

The pilot will initially use test or historical customer inquiries rather than a live customer inbox.

AlphaGraphics will provide representative email threads relating to business card estimate requests. AltOps will use these scenarios to configure, test, and refine the agent.

Once the workflow is demonstrated successfully, live email integration and additional product categories can be considered as a separate next phase.

In the pilot

  • Business card estimate requests
  • Historical or test email threads
  • Corebridge test or sandbox environment
  • Draft estimates for human review

Later, after the pilot

  • Live customer inbox integration
  • Additional product categories
  • Additional locations
  • Broader estimating automation

4What we need from you

To complete the pilot, AlphaGraphics will provide the following.

  • Access to a Corebridge test, sandbox, or approved MIS environment.
  • Representative historical customer estimate email threads.
  • Basic guidance regarding standard business-card specifications and estimating practices.
  • Assistance from the appropriate MIS administrator where required.
No passwords needed

AlphaGraphics does not need to provide passwords directly to AltOps. Where possible, AlphaGraphics personnel log into Corebridge and other required platforms directly through the secure AltOps Agent Computer. The authenticated session is then used by the agent for testing.

If additional platforms become necessary during the agreed pilot workflow, the required access will be discussed before they are connected.

5Human review

The pilot agent will operate as an estimating assistant, not an autonomous estimator.

Any estimate created by the agent will remain in draft or review status and will require review by an AlphaGraphics employee before being provided to a customer.

8Platform security

Section 20 of your scope of work asks how customer data, credentials, and email content are protected. This is a summary of how the AltOps platform handles that today. Full documentation comes with the full proposal.

AltOps owns the platform and carries the security responsibility for it: the controls, the compliance program, and the response if something goes wrong.

Your data stays separate from every other client

  • Every company gets its own isolated environment, and every individual user inside that company gets their own isolated execution pod with its own private storage.
  • Network rules block traffic between clients by default. One client's pods cannot reach another client's pods.
  • There is no shared memory, knowledge, or prompt content across clients. When a run starts, the agent is shown only your company's rules, data, and tools.
  • Every database row is stamped with the company and user it belongs to, and the database enforces this separation itself, not just the application.
  • Fully private deployment is available: dedicated infrastructure, or on-premise inside your own environment.

Passwords and logins

  • AlphaGraphics staff log into Corebridge and email themselves, through the secure AltOps Agent Computer. AltOps never needs your passwords.
  • Logged-in sessions live only inside that one person's pod, on an encrypted volume. The platform database holds no plaintext credentials.
  • Sessions are per person. An agent working for one user cannot inherit another user's access.
  • Where a system supports it, we use OAuth, so access can be revoked at any time without changing a password.

Data protection

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit. All stored data is encrypted at rest.
  • All data is stored on US infrastructure.
  • Automated backups with point-in-time recovery.
  • Client data is never used to train any AI model, by AltOps or by our model providers. We operate under commercial API terms where submitted data is not used for training.
  • Client data is never used to improve another client's agents.

Audit trail

Section 19 of your scope of work asks for a complete history of AI activity. The platform records this for every run.

  • Every step in sequence, with a screenshot of every screen the agent touched.
  • Every tool call with its inputs and outputs.
  • The exact model version and the exact instructions and business rules in force for that run.
  • Every human intervention, correction, and approval, with who and when.
  • Run records are frozen at execution time and cannot be rewritten afterwards.

Human control

  • Any automation can be paused instantly from the admin console. Work then goes back to the person who did it before.
  • A run cannot report success if it skipped a record. A silent partial success is treated as a failure, not a pass.
  • Anything involving a number or a decision with money attached is handled by deterministic code, not by the AI model.
